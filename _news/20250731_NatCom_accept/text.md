Our collaborative paper on voltage-gated ion transport in Ångström-scale confinement has been accepted for publication in Nature Communications. Congratulations to Dr. Biswabhusan and our collaborators.

The study demonstrates voltage-gated transport through channels 2.6–6 Å wide, with nearly complete cation selectivity even at high concentration.
