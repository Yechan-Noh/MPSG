Research

I use molecular dynamics and physical models to study transport at interfaces and through nanoscale pores. My research examines how molecular interactions, fluctuations, and confinement give rise to selective transport, mechanosensitivity, and memory.

At NIST, I investigated mechanosensitive ion transport and computationally demonstrated synaptic-like plasticity arising from competition between ionic species. At Notre Dame, I lead molecular-simulation investigations in collaboration with experimental groups working on biosensing and biomaterials. My long-term aim is to discover new transport phenomena, explain their physical origins, and develop principles for molecular biomimetic engineering.
